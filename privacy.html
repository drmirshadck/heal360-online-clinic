Privacy

How Heal 360 Online Clinic looks after your details.

What we ask for

When you book, we ask for your name, email, mobile number, an optional note about your concern, and your UPI payment reference. If your doctor advises medicines, we also keep the medicine amount and your payment reference for that.

Why we ask

  • To book your time with your doctor and send your confirmation and video link.
  • To send reminders and booking updates by email and, once switched on, by WhatsApp in English and Malayalam.
  • To work out whether your booking is a new consultation or a follow-up, using your mobile number.
  • To let your doctor see your booking and your note before the consultation.

How it is kept

Your name, email, mobile number, note and payment reference are stored in encrypted form and are visible only to the doctor you booked and the clinic. Bookings are deleted automatically about one year after the appointment.

Video consultations

Your video room is private to your booking. Calls are not recorded and nothing from the call is stored on our side.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your details for advertising.
  • Payments are made by UPI directly to your doctor. We never see or store your bank or card details.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your booking details at any time. Call +91 75111 36045 or email care@heal360homoeopathy.com.
