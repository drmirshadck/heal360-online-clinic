Have questions before you book?

Questions.
Answers.

How does an online consultation work?

You book a time, receive a private video link by email, and join from your phone or computer. Your doctor speaks with you, asks about your history and advises a plan.

Which doctor should I choose?

Dr. Seena is the specialist for children and allergies, Dr. Shameema for nerve pain, carpal tunnel syndrome, neck pain and sciatica, and Dr. Musheera for skin and hair health. If you are unsure, send a WhatsApp message and we will guide you.

Is an online consultation right for emergencies?

No. Online consultations are not for emergencies. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in India) or go to the nearest hospital.

What are the fees?

A new consultation is ₹300. A follow-up within 7 days of your consultation is free, and a follow-up on days 8 to 14 is ₹150. From the 15th day a new consultation applies.

How do I pay?

You pay by UPI while booking. Scan the QR code or tap Pay in your UPI app, and the payment goes straight to your doctor. Your doctor confirms the booking after seeing the payment.

Will my medicines reach me at home?

Yes. We offer a courier service, so your medicines are delivered right to your door.

What if my doctor advises medicines?

Your doctor adds the medicine amount for you. You then receive a payment link by email, and the payment goes to that doctor's own UPI. Your booking page shows the amount and lets you tell us when you have paid.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Use the Change or cancel link in your booking email. Changes close a short time before the consultation, so please do it early.

Is my consultation private?

Yes. Your video room is private to your booking and the call is not recorded.
