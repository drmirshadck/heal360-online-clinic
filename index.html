Questions families ask

How does an online consultation work?

You book a time, receive a private video link by email, and join from your phone or computer. Your doctor speaks with you, asks about your health and routine, and explains the treatment plan.

Which doctor should I choose?

Dr. Seena is the specialist for children and allergies, Dr. Shameema for carpal tunnel syndrome and hand numbness, and Dr. Musheera for skin and hair concerns. If you are unsure, call or WhatsApp the clinic and we will guide you.

Is an online consultation right for emergencies?

No. Online consultations are not for emergencies. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in India) or go to the nearest hospital.

Is my consultation private?

Yes. Your video room is private to your booking and the call is not recorded.
